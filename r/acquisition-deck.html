Buying a Business in Canada, in 12 Slides

How to find, value, finance and screen an established small business, with one worked deal.

Use ← → keys or swipe

01 · Why buy

You buy proven demand

  • Customers, payroll and pricing already work
  • Over 75% of Canadian small business owners plan to exit within the decade; fewer than 9% have a succession plan [6]
  • AI removes costs; it rarely creates the margin. Underwrite the deal without it

02 · The target

What to look for

  • Revenue $500K to $5M, SDE $150K to $750K, 3+ years of steady profit
  • No customer above 15% to 20% of revenue
  • Admin-heavy, repeat revenue, non-technical competitors
  • The owner is the bottleneck, not the reason customers come
2.61x

Average multiple of seller's discretionary earnings (SDE) in the US-derived market baseline; median SDE $165K, median price $350K [3]

03 · Valuation

Multiples by business type

Multiples rise with recurring revenue and a manager in place; they fall with owner dependence and concentration.

04 · Financing

The Canadian stack

  • CSBFP: up to $1M term + $150K line; max $150K for intangibles and working capital; no share purchases; capped at prime + 3% [1]
  • BDC: lends against goodwill and cash flow, share deals allowed [2]
  • Vendor take-back: seller carries part of the price over 3 to 5 years
  • Equity: 10% to 25% real equity; BDC guides 20% to 30% down
$250K of $525K

All CSBFP can legally carry on a $700K mostly-goodwill asset deal. The rest must come from BDC, a bank or the seller

05 · The test

DSCR of 1.25x, on the right number

  • Canadian banks: 1.25x minimum, 1.35x preferred. US SBA: 1.15x
  • Compute it on Adjusted EBITDA = SDE minus a market salary for the buyer
  • Using SDE overstates coverage by one full salary

06 · Worked deal (illustrative)

A $600K service business

  • SDE $200K, minus $75K salary = $125K Adjusted EBITDA
  • $150K equity, $90K VTB at 6% over 5 years, $360K senior over 7 years ($250K CSBFP at 7.45%, $110K BDC at 8.45%)
  • Debt service about $87,700 a year
  • DSCR 1.43x: financeable. On SDE it would read 2.28x

07 · Worked deal (illustrative)

Price decides financeability

Same business, same stack shares. At $700K it falls to 1.22x and fails a bank screen, while SDE still shows 1.95x.

08 · Screening

Screen hard, trust little

  • Funnel: skim 100, analyze 50, offer on 10, close 1 [5]
  • 57% of deals had SDE overstated by 5%+; 19% by over 25% [4]
  • About 65% of failed acquisitions traced to the seller relationship [4]
  • Be wary of "runs itself", "turnkey", "must sell", "100% seller financing"
