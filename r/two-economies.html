A Tale of Two Economies

A few AI-linked companies carry a large share of US stock value and investment growth. Consumers, housing and hiring tell a weaker story.

Use ← → keys or swipe

01 · Market value

35%

Share of the S&P 500 held by seven tech companies: Nvidia, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta and Tesla [1]

02 · Market value

The ten largest weights

Percent of the S&P 500, 19 October 2026. Alphabet's two share classes are combined [1].

03 · Returns

Most of the gains came from AI names

  • From ChatGPT's launch in Nov 2022 to Sep 2025, AI-related stocks delivered 75% of S&P 500 returns and 80% of its earnings growth [2].
  • Seven AI-oriented stocks produced over half of the index's total gain in 2025 [2].
  • Nvidia alone is now about 7.5% of the index [1].

04 · Spending

The building boom

  • Alphabet raised its 2026 capital spending plan to $195-205 billion, from $180-190 billion [3].
  • Meta's 2026 plan is $125-145 billion, up from $115-135 billion [4].
  • Reported combined 2026 plans for Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet and Meta top $700 billion. This is a press tally, not company guidance [4].

05 · GDP

AI investment held up 2025 growth

Real GDP growth vs the contribution from AI-related investment (equipment, software, R&D, data centers), annualized percentage points [5].

Over the first nine months of 2025 these categories made up 39% of growth, vs 28% in 2000 at the dot-com peak [5].

06 · GDP, 2026

A third to a half of growth, by definition

ING's estimates of tech investment's share of year-over-year GDP growth, Q2 2026 [6].

ING says even its narrowest measure likely overstates the AI part, since old and new tech spending are hard to separate [6].

07 · The import catch

Much of the hardware is imported

Contribution to Q2 2026 year-over-year growth (2.1%), percentage points [7].

Hardware imports rose from 0.5% to 1.6% of GDP since 2024 [7].

08 · Other investment

Tech crowds out the rest

  • Real tech investment is up 62% since Q1 2022 [6].
  • Business investment outside tech fell year over year for six straight quarters, Q4 2024 to Q1 2026 [6].
  • Tech imports tripled in two years to about $60 billion a month [6].

09 · Consumers

Spending, but not feeling good

  • Michigan consumer sentiment fell to 46.3 in early October, from 48.1. One-year inflation expectations rose to 4.7% [8].
  • Households save 2.7% of disposable income, vs a 5.2% average over 2000-2019 [7].
  • ING estimates stock-market wealth adds about 0.44 points to GDP through spending, led by high-income households [6].

11 · Jobs

Hiring has nearly stalled

September 2026 job change by sector, thousands. Total: +29,000. Unemployment: 4.2%. July was revised to a loss of 10,000 [10].

ING notes entry-level graduate hiring is down 17% since 2019 [6].

12 · The link

Why the two economies are tied

  • Q2 2026 GDP grew 2.2%. BEA's upward revision to structures was led by data centers [11].
  • Consumer spending still adds more to growth than AI hardware and software, before imports: 1.53 vs 0.70 points [7].
  • The main risk: a fall in AI stock prices cuts wealth, then spending by the households doing most of it [7].
